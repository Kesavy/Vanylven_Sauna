Pay with Vipps

  1. 1

    Open Vipps and tap Pay.

  2. 2

    Search for 943550. That is our Vipps number.

  3. 3

    Enter the amount: 600 kr for a private session, 100 kr per person for open sauna.

  4. 4

    Add a message (see below) and confirm.

Vipps number
943550

In the message, write the date of your session and your name, for example 12 June, Kari Nordmann. That is how we match your payment to your booking.

Scan to pay

Point your phone camera at the code and Vipps opens with our number filled in.

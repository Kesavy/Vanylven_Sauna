Privacy

We collect as little as we can, and only what we need to run the sauna. Here is what that means.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who is responsible

Vanylven Sauna in Fiskå, run by volunteers from Vanylven, is responsible for the personal details described here. Contact us at vanylvensauna@gmail.com.

Gift card orders

When you order a gift card on this website, we receive your email address, the names you write on the card (to and from), the message, the number of hours and the time of the order.

  • Why: to issue the gift card, send it to you and check it when it is used.
  • Legal basis: it is needed to deliver what you ordered (GDPR article 6 (1) b). We do not ask for consent and we do not use your email for anything else, such as newsletters.
  • How long: until the gift card has expired, one year after the order. Then we delete the order.

Bookings and payments

Bookings are made in Calendly, which receives the name and email you enter there. Payments go through Vipps or your bank. We use these details only to handle your booking and match your payment, and we do not keep them longer than we need to.

Who else handles the data

  • Vercel stores gift card orders for us. The data is kept in Frankfurt, Germany. Vercel is an American company and follows the EU rules for transfers to the USA.
  • Calendly handles bookings, Vipps handles mobile payments.
  • We never sell or share your details with anyone else.

When you visit the website

The website itself has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. To show the page, your browser fetches fonts from Google Fonts and icons and scripts from public content networks (unpkg and cdnjs). These services see your IP address when your browser asks for the files, as any website does. The map on the Find us page is embedded from Google Maps; when it loads, Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, under the Google privacy policy. The host of this website also keeps short technical logs.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we have about you, have them corrected or deleted, or object to how we use them. Write to vanylvensauna@gmail.com. If you think we handle your details wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

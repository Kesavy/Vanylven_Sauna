Give a few quiet hours

A gift card is something to do, not something to keep. Fill in the card and see it take shape, then send us the order.

How long?

This opens your email app with the order filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

How it works

  1. 1

    Send the order from your email.

  2. 2

    Pay with Vipps or bank transfer. Our Vipps number is 943550. Payment details

  3. 3

    We send you the gift card by email, with its own number, ready to print or forward.

  4. 4

    To use it, the recipient books a time as usual and emails us the gift card number instead of paying.

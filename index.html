Step into the steam.

A small sauna on the rocks of the Vanylvsfjord in Fiskå. Heat up, swim in the sea, slow down.

Book a session See prices

On the rocks below Fiskå, a small sauna looks out over the Vanylvsfjord. It is run by volunteers from Vanylven, and it is open to anyone who wants to feel the heat, the cold sea, and the calm that comes after.

  3. Steam

1 of 6

Arrive

Walk down to the shore below Fiskå Brygge Gjestgiveri. Enter your door code. Leave the day outside.

2 of 6

Heat

The warmth comes slowly, then all at once. An electric stove, wooden benches, and the fjord in the window.

3 of 6

Steam

Ladle water over the stones. A wave of steam rolls down from the ceiling. Breathe it in.

4 of 6

Cold

Then out, down the deck and into the Vanylvsfjord. Every part of you wakes up at once.

5 of 6

Rest

Back by the big window, wrapped in a towel. Nothing to do but watch the water.

6 of 6

Again

Two hours is time for a few rounds. Heat, cold, rest. Repeat until you are completely still.

Before you come

A few simple rules, so everyone finds the sauna the way they hoped to.

Bring

  • Swimwear
  • Your own towel to sit or lie on
  • Plenty of water to drink before, during and after

In the sauna

  • Only fresh water on the stones
  • No food inside the sauna
  • No alcohol, inside or outside
  • Leave it as you would like to find it

Good to know

  • Maximum 8 people at a time
  • Children under 16 come with an adult
  • Sauna and swimming are at your own risk

Give a few quiet hours

A gift card is something to do, not something to keep. You choose the number of hours.

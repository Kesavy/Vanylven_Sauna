Right at the water's edge

The sauna sits on the rocks below Fiskå Brygge Gjestgiveri, with big windows facing the Vanylvsfjord. A few steps from the bench, you are in the sea.

Built the sauna

Arrive

Follow the shore below the guesthouse to the cabin on the rocks, and let yourself in with your door code.

Heat

Close the door, ladle water over the stones and let the steam roll over you.

Cold

Walk down the deck and into the Vanylvsfjord. Breathe out. Then back into the warmth.

Rest

Sit by the big window and watch the light on the water. Then go again.

Two hours is time for a few rounds

Before you come

A few simple rules, so everyone finds the sauna the way they hoped to.

Bring

  • Swimwear
  • Your own towel to sit or lie on
  • Plenty of water to drink before, during and after

In the sauna

  • Only fresh water on the stones
  • No food inside the sauna
  • No alcohol, inside or outside
  • Leave it as you would like to find it

Good to know

  • Maximum 8 people at a time
  • Children under 16 come with an adult
  • Sauna and swimming are at your own risk

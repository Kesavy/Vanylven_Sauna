Getting here

  1. 1

    Head for Fiskå Brygge Gjestgiveri

    The sauna is in the centre of Fiskå, by the harbour. The directions buttons above take you there.

  2. 2

    Walk down to the shore

    The sauna sits on the rocks right below the guesthouse, at the water’s edge.

  3. 3

    Enter your door code

    For private sessions, the code comes by email the day before. Open sauna on Wednesdays needs no booking.
